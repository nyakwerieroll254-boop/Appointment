THE SERVICE

Learn computing one clear step at a time.

Computing opens doors when the concepts feel approachable. In a personal lecture, focus on the topic that matters to you, work through it at a comfortable pace, and connect the explanation to practical examples you can relate to.

Sessions cost KSh 3,000 per hour. Select one to four consecutive hours when booking; your total price and appointment length update to match your choice. Include the subject you want to cover in the booking note to help prepare a focused lesson.

Learning can include

  • Computer fundamentals and digital literacy
  • How software, files, and devices work together
  • Introduction to programming and computational thinking
  • Breaking down a topic that has felt difficult
  • Guided practice and time to ask questions

A flexible way to learn

Whether you’re starting out or revisiting a specific concept, sessions adapt to your goals. Tell me your experience level and what you hope to leave the lesson understanding.
