BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few useful details.

When are appointments?

Weekdays with hourly starts from 7:00 AM; every appointment finishes by 7:00 PM in your local timezone. The calendar adjusts available start times to the session length.

How are lessons priced?

Robotics is KSh 2,000 per hour. Computing is KSh 3,000 per hour. Select one to four consecutive hours to see the total before requesting.

How is web development priced?

The listed fee is KSh 20,000 per project. Your booking is a 60-minute consultation to discuss goals and confirm project scope before work begins.

Is a deposit required?

No. You can confirm a booking immediately without paying a deposit. If you prefer to pay a 50% deposit, use M-Pesa or online checkout when available. Manual payments are verified before confirmation.
