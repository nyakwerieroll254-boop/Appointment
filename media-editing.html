THE SERVICE

Make your content easier to watch and remember.

Editing is where individual clips become a complete piece. Whether you’re creating a video, preparing content for online audiences, or need help shaping existing footage, focused editing time can improve how the story flows from start to finish.

Editing support is priced at KSh 1,500 per hour, with a one-hour appointment. The booking is for editing work and discussion; before we begin, agree the material, intended format, deliverables, and estimated time needed for your project.

Editing support can focus on

  • Reviewing footage and deciding what supports the story
  • Improving the structure, rhythm, and flow
  • Removing distracting pauses and tightening sequences
  • Preparing an edit for its intended platform and audience
  • Discussing feedback and practical next steps

What to share before your session

Use the booking note to describe your project, the kind of media you have, the result you’re aiming for, and your intended platform. We can confirm the best way to share footage and agree on deliverables before editing begins.
