THE SERVICE

Understand the ideas behind intelligent machines.

Robotics is easier to understand when the pieces make sense together. In a guided lecture, explore how sensors provide information, how logic and code guide decisions, and how outputs create movement or action. Build understanding at a pace that leaves room for questions.

Sessions are priced at KSh 2,000 per hour. Choose one, two, three, or four consecutive hours when booking. The selected hours determine both the total fee and the reserved lesson duration.

Topics we can explore

  • What the main components of a robotic system do
  • Inputs, sensors, and how a robot detects its surroundings
  • Logic, instructions, and programming fundamentals
  • Outputs, movement, and responding to events
  • Breaking a practical robotics challenge into smaller steps

Who it’s for

Students, beginners, curious makers, and anyone looking for a clearer starting point in robotics. Share your current experience and what you’d like to learn in the booking note so the session can be tailored to your goals.
