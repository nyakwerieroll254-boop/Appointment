HOW I WORK

Good work should feel clear and collaborative.

Technology can be exciting without being intimidating. A website should be useful, a lesson should leave you more confident, and an edit should make your story easier to follow.

My work spans web development, robotics and computing instruction, and media editing. Each service is different, but the approach is consistent: understand what you’re aiming for, explain the options clearly, and focus on progress you can see.

Start by exploring the services, then book a weekday time. Lecture sessions can be booked from one to four hours. If you’re unsure where to begin, send me a note and tell me a little about your idea.
